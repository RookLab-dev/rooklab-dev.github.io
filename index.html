About

RookLab is an independent software company focused on quantitative trading infrastructure for digital asset markets. We are building tools for the parts of systematic trading that developers usually have to assemble themselves: market data, strategy development, backtesting, risk management and execution.

RookLab is founder-led by Rook0z, a Python developer who built Hyper-Engine, the technical foundation behind the company.

Market Data

Reliable infrastructure for working with digital-asset market data.

Existing

Market data ingestion for Hyperliquid.

Planned

Historical and real-time trades, candles and funding rates, with order-book data to be evaluated.

Trading Systems

Tools for building and deploying systematic trading strategies.

Existing

Strategy signals, backtesting and strategy comparison, with indicators written from scratch rather than taken from third-party technical-analysis libraries.

Planned

A backtesting engine that accounts for fees, slippage, position sizing and execution assumptions.

Risk & Execution

Infrastructure for managing risk and executing strategies safely.

Existing

Position sizing, daily loss limits, a maximum number of open positions, order signing and execution on Hyperliquid testnet.

Planned

Exposure and drawdown controls, order management and exchange connectivity.

Quantitative Tooling

Validation and analysis for systematic strategies.

Existing

Out-of-sample testing, walk-forward validation, Monte Carlo trade simulation and Sharpe-based strategy selection.

Planned

Parameter sensitivity, robustness testing and regime analysis.

Performance

Python for research and prototyping.

Future  Rust for performance-critical systems.

How we plan to use AI

Future  An interface that turns a described hypothesis into a structured experiment. Calculations, backtests and statistics stay in deterministic Python and Rust code. A language model never produces the results.
